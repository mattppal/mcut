---
title: "Zoom regions"
description: "Punch-ins as one object with in, hold, and out timing, a target, easing, and motion blur."
---

A zoom region is one punch-in on a video, image, or multicam element. It zooms
in over `inMs`, holds for `holdMs`, and zooms back out over `outMs`, all in
element-local time. A multicam zoom names a `source` key, so every slot showing
that source zooms and the other slots keep their framing. The screen can zoom
while the camera overlay stays put. A slot zooms inside its crop, so a zoom
never shows source the crop cuts away. A multicam zoom with no `source` zooms
the whole composite, overlays included, inside the element's frame.

Regions on one target never overlap. The zoom commands keep the region they add
or change inside the element. A split copies a region that crosses the cut into
both halves, so the picture on each side stays the same. The right half's copy
gets the id with `-r` appended, so no two pieces share a zoom id. A trim can
leave a region running past an edge, where it plays cut off. Growing a clip
from its start shifts the regions with the content.

```ts
engine.dispatch({
  type: 'addZoomRegion',
  elementId: 'e-multicam',
  zoom: { preset: 'detailZoom', source: 'screen', atMs: 12_000, focus: { x: 0.75, y: 0.3 } },
})
```

## Types

### ZoomRegion

`ZoomRegion` is `z.infer<typeof zoomRegionSchema>`, one stored zoom region with every field resolved.

### ZoomRegionRef

`ZoomRegionRef` extends `ZoomRegion` with `elementId` and the region's timeline `startMs` and `endMs`, cut at the element's edges. A region that crosses a split lists the part each piece plays.

### ZoomableElement

`ZoomableElement` is a video, image, or multicam element, the element types that hold zoom regions.

### ContentView

`ContentView` is `{ scale, focus }`, how a clip or slot frames its source at one
instant. `scale` multiplies the fit scale. `focus` is the anchor point from 0 to
1 that stays fixed while it scales.

### VisibleFraction

`VisibleFraction` is `{ x, y }`, the share of a slot's cropped source each axis
shows at scale 1 after the slot's fit. A cover slot shows less than 1 on the
axis it cuts, and a contain slot shows more than 1 on the axis it letterboxes.
`getSlotView` and `centeredFocus` take it.

## Schemas

### zoomRegionSchema

| Field | Type | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | string | Stable id, minted as `z-...` when omitted |
| `source` | string, optional | Multicam source key whose slots zoom. Absent on clips, and absent on a multicam zoom of the whole composite |
| `atMs` | integer ms | Element-local start of the zoom-in, negative on the right half of a split through a zoom |
| `inMs`, `holdMs`, `outMs` | integer ms | Ramp in, hold, ramp out |
| `focus` | `{ x, y }` from 0 to 1 | Target center, across the cropped frame of a clip or multicam slot |
| `scale` | 1 to 8 | Scale at the hold |
| `easing` | `Easing` | Applied to both ramps |
| `motionBlur` | 0 to 1 | Shutter as a fraction of one frame while the zoom moves. 0 turns it off |

### zoomCommandSchema

`zoomCommandSchema` is the discriminated union of `addZoomRegion`, `updateZoomRegion`, and `removeZoomRegion` with their `type`. It is the input of the MCP `edit_zooms` tool.

## Presets

### ZOOM_REGION_PRESETS

| Preset | Scale | In | Hold | Out |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `subtlePunchIn` | 1.15 | 700 ms | 1600 ms | 700 ms |
| `detailZoom` | 1.3 | 600 ms | 3000 ms | 600 ms |

`addZoomRegion` defaults to `subtlePunchIn`, `easeOutExpo`, and motion blur 0.5.
Explicit fields override the preset. A `rect` aims the zoom at its center and fills it only up to the preset scale, so a region never makes a detail zoom severe. Pass `focus` and `scale` for a stronger zoom. The MCP tools warn when any zoom goes above 1.5x.

## Functions

### listZoomRegions

`listZoomRegions(project)` returns every zoom region in the project as `ZoomRegionRef[]`, in track and element order.

### isZoomable

`isZoomable(element)` returns `true` for a video, image, or multicam element and narrows it to `ZoomableElement`.

### zoomRegionEndMs

`zoomRegionEndMs(region)` returns the element-local time a zoom region ends, `atMs + inMs + holdMs + outMs`.

### getSlotView

`getSlotView(element, slot, timelineMs, visible, rest)` returns the `ContentView` of one multicam layout slot. It is the one place a renderer reads a slot's framing. `visible` is the `VisibleFraction` of the slot, which differs from 1 when the slot's shape differs from its crop, and it defaults to the full frame. `rest` is the focus outside a zoom, where a zoom starts and ends, and it defaults to the center. The compositor passes the reframed focus from `centeredFocus` for a source with a reframe track. With neither a zoom nor a track, the view is the center at scale 1, so the slot's crop alone sets its framing.

### getClipView

`getClipView(element, timelineMs)` returns the `ContentView` of a video or image clip inside its crop, or of a whole multicam composite from its zooms with no `source`.

### getZoomedRect

`getZoomedRect(view, rect)` maps a normalized frame rect through a whole-composite `ContentView`. It returns where the rect lands on the canvas when the composite shows the view's 1/scale crop window at its anchored focus. `flattenMulticam` bakes it into each flattened clip's transform keyframes. The multicam renderer composes its slots unzoomed and draws that window of the composed frame, the way a clip draws its `getClipView` window, so a flattened clip follows its slot exactly.

### getZoomShutterMs

`getZoomShutterMs(element, timelineMs, frameMs)` returns the motion blur shutter in milliseconds while any zoom on the element ramps, and 0 while every zoom holds or rests. The compositor renders the element at views sampled across this window into a scratch canvas and composites the average once, the same path as keyframe motion blur.
