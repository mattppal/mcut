Add zoom regions, one punch-in object with in, hold, and out timing, a target, easing, and motion blur, on a clip or on one multicam source. Adds the `addZoomRegion`, `updateZoomRegion`, and `removeZoomRegion` commands, `getSlotView` and `getClipView`, compositor rendering with motion blur while a zoom moves, and the `list_zooms` and `edit_zooms` MCP tools.
